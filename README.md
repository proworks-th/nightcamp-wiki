# NightCamp Wiki

Live: https://proworks-th.github.io/nightcamp-wiki/

Fan-made wiki and tools for the Thai MMORPG **NightCamp**, by KhumGameLab. `index.html` is the top bar; each page (`guide.html`, `equip.html`, `sim.html`, `patch.html`) loads the first time its tab opens, pictures live in `img/`, and the English dictionary (`i18n.js`) loads only in English mode. Open it through the website (it needs a web server, not a double-click).

- **ค้นหา / Search** (box in the top bar, or press `/`): one search over everything — items and their stats (`crit`, `ตีเร็ว`, `hp`…), monsters (opens what they drop), random options, jobs, skills, leveling spots, guide tips and patch notes. The index is `search.json`, fetched the first time the box is used.
- **คู่มือ / Guide** (`#guide`): your character at a glance, where to level by map and element, every job, and the game systems worth knowing.
- **อุปกรณ์ / Equipment** (`#equip`): 1,396 weapons (including the starter weapons and the 20 root-possessed bosses' weapons), 80 armour sets, 80 hats, 28 glasses and 9 shields. Opens on all categories (filter by weapon, armour, hat, glasses or shield); search by item, stat, monster, map, material or ability; drop chance at your level; craft recipes; send an item to the Build Sim.
- **Build Sim** (`#sim`): every job's skill tree, stats, gear slots, compare two builds, share codes (`NCB1.…`); a link ending in `#sim/NCB1.…` opens that build.

Set your job and level once in the top bar and every page follows it. Press EN / ไทย in the top bar to switch language (English names are fan translations, not official).

Based on the NightCamp test build of 21 Oct 2026 (game-20261021-1537); numbers may change with patches.
Game names, art and data belong to the Night Camp team; the logo, key art and job portraits come from the Night Camp press kit and are used with permission. This is a fan site, not an official one.
